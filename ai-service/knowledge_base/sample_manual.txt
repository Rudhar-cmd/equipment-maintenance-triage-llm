Industrial Compressor Maintenance Manual

Section: Bearing and vibration inspection
Page: 1
Inspect bearings when vibration or temperature exceeds operating limits.
Check abnormal heat, unusual noise, lubrication problems, visible damage,
loose mounting, alignment problems, and mechanical imbalance.

Section: Temperature
Page: 2
Sustained motor temperature above 90°C should be investigated.
Temperature alone does not establish the root cause.

Section: Vibration
Page: 3
Vibration above 7 mm/s should be investigated. Possible contributors include
mechanical imbalance, misalignment, loose mounting, or bearing problems.
These are possible causes and require technician inspection before confirmation.

Section: Maintenance response
Page: 4
Document readings, inspect the affected assembly, record findings, and obtain
technician approval before maintenance work is authorized.
